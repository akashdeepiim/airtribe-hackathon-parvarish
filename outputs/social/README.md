# Parvarish social story

- `parvarish-story.png`: 1080 × 1920 artwork for Instagram Stories, Facebook Stories or WhatsApp Status.
- `parvarish-story.svg`: editable vector source with embedded Fraunces and DM Sans fonts.
- `parvarish-story.html`: self-contained browser preview.

Suggested story link sticker: **Find your chapter** → https://parvarish.akash-deep.com

Optional caption: They grow. You grow, too. 🌱 From the first “what if” to the fifth birthday — find your chapter with Parvarish.

Alt text: Cream and forest-green Parvarish artwork with a smiling illustrated child in golden dungarees, a sun, clouds and a sprout. The headline reads “They grow. You grow, too.” Below: “No perfect parents. Just your kind of family.”

The artwork reuses the project's original illustrations and licensed brand fonts. Font licences are in `public/fonts/`. Regenerate using `scripts/build-social-story.mjs`; set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE_PATH` to an available Playwright installation and `CHROME_PATH` if Chrome is installed elsewhere.
