# Sign in and create your child's space

Document: PAR-KB-02 | Version: 1.0 | Reviewed: 24 October 2026

Scope: Account and profile help for the reviewed Parvarish web implementation. Exact controls can differ in other releases.

## How do I sign in to Parvarish?

1. Open Sign in and enter your email address.
2. Select Send me a code.
3. Check your email for the six-digit code. Enter it in Email code.
4. Select Verify and continue.

The same flow creates an account for a new email. Parvarish currently uses email codes, not phone OTP or a password. Never send your sign-in code to the chatbot or another person.

## What if my email code does not work?

The email code is valid for 10 minutes. Check the address you entered and your spam or junk folder. Use the most recently requested code. Send another code becomes available after a 60-second wait. Use a different email lets you correct the address.

If a code has expired, request another one. If requests are temporarily limited, wait before trying again. A failed sign-in does not by itself mean your child profile has been deleted. The chatbot cannot read your inbox, retrieve a code or unlock an account.

## How do I create a child profile?

1. Sign in and open your family space.
2. Select Create a child profile. If you already have a profile, select Add another child.
3. Review the adult parent or lawful guardian declaration and the privacy choices. Only confirm a declaration that is true for you.
4. Enable Memories to continue. Make an explicit choice for Milestones, Health records, Personalisation and Notifications; these four are optional.
5. Enter the child's details and select Create private profile.

Creating a profile requires Memories consent. You can choose Not now for each optional purpose. Enabling a future purpose does not make its feature available today.

## Which child profile details are required?

Child's name, Date of birth and City are required in the current form. Enter the date as YYYY-MM-DD; a future date is not accepted. The sign-up experience is intended for children aged 0 to 5. Child profiles begin after birth, so do not enter an expected due date as a date of birth.

A few things they love is optional. Separate interests with commas, for example, music, picture books, pretend play. Sex is optional and can remain Not specified. The form explains that sex is reserved for future growth charts and will not determine activity suggestions. A profile photograph is optional and can be added after creation.

The current privacy notice asks for demonstration information while launch work is completed. This guide does not change that notice.

## How do I edit a profile or add a photograph?

Open the child's space and select Edit profile, make your changes, then select Save profile. Cancel leaves the editing flow without saving. Profile-edit permission is required; owners and co-parents have it by default, and the owner manages permitted access changes.

Use Add profile photo or Change photo on the child's page. Photo changes require profile-edit permission and Memories consent. Photos are resized and compressed for upload. This control changes one profile photo; it is not a memory timeline or bulk upload tool.

If an upload fails, check your connection and try a standard image from your device. If photo controls are absent, check permissions and Privacy choices before assuming the image is the problem.

## How do I switch between children?

Return to your family space and choose Open [child's name]'s space for the relevant profile. Each child has separate family access and privacy choices. Access to one child does not automatically give access to another.

## What if a child profile is missing or unavailable?

Check that you signed in with the email used to create the profile or accept its invitation. If you are a family member, ask the owner to check your membership and permissions. Try refreshing the family list. An unavailable profile can reflect changed access or a loading problem; the chatbot cannot determine that it was deleted.
