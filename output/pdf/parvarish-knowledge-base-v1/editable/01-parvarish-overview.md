# Parvarish: what you can do today

Document: PAR-KB-01 | Version: 1.0 | Reviewed: 24 October 2026

Scope: Platform help for the web implementation reviewed on this date. The deployed version may differ; check the controls visible in your account.

## What is Parvarish?

Parvarish is a family platform being built around children's everyday moments, firsts and the people who care for them. The current web experience provides private child profiles, family access settings, purpose-specific privacy choices and a signed-in chatbot called Ask Parvarish.

The public website includes family journeys and a journal. These can be read without an account. The public journeys cover planning a family, pregnancy and a child's first five years. Choosing a public chapter changes the page; it does not create a pregnancy profile or save a due date. Child profiles begin after birth.

## Which Parvarish features are available in this version?

- Sign in or create an account with a six-digit code sent to your email.
- Create more than one child profile and open each child's separate space.
- Add and edit basic profile details, and add or change a profile photograph when permitted.
- Invite a family member using a private, email-bound invitation link.
- Manage family roles, individual access settings and pending invitations according to your permissions.
- Review and change five separate privacy purposes.
- Use Ask Parvarish after signing in, where the chat service is configured.

The chat can explain steps and offer ideas. It does not carry out account or family actions for you.

## Are memory timelines, milestones and health records ready?

Memory timelines, bulk photo uploads, milestone tracking, downloadable memory templates, health-record forms and the personalised activity feed are not available in the reviewed interface. The child page describes memories, milestones and activities as coming next.

A profile photo is available; a full memory album is not. The public journal contains editorial stories; it is not a private family journal editor. Chat can suggest a memory prompt or draft a caption for you to copy, but it cannot save a memory to Parvarish.

## Why do I see consent or permissions for a future feature?

Parvarish already has choices and access settings for purposes such as health records and milestones. These settings describe consent and eligibility. They do not mean that the corresponding screen, reminder or storage workflow is available.

Enabling personalisation does not give this version of Ask Parvarish access to your child's profile, photos or health information. The chat uses what you type in the conversation.

## Are notifications, mobile apps and deletion available?

Web notifications are off in this version. Mobile reminders and separate device permissions are planned. iOS and Android experiences are not confirmed as released; the reviewed experience is web first.

Account export, full child-data deletion, storage cleanup and deletion receipts are not available in this interface. Withdrawing a privacy choice or removing a family member is not the same as deleting stored data.

## Is Parvarish a medical or developmental service?

Ask Parvarish can offer general, non-clinical parenting support: simple play, everyday connection, gentle routines and memory prompts. It does not diagnose, assess development, interpret health records, prescribe treatment or provide fertility or pregnancy advice. It cannot guarantee a child's safety or a developmental outcome.

For questions about symptoms, medicines, growth, feeding, sleep safety or developmental concerns, speak with an appropriately qualified professional. If someone may be in immediate danger, seek local emergency help directly instead of waiting for chat.

## Where can I find support, prices or a release date?

This knowledge base has no confirmed support email, response-time commitment, pricing plan, final retention schedule or dates for future features. Use the contact details on the current Parvarish privacy page if they are published there. Do not assume an address, price or release date from a chatbot answer.

The current privacy page describes Parvarish as an early version and asks people to use demonstration information while hosted-service and export/deletion work is completed.
